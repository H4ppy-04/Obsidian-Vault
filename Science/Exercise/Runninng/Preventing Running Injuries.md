
### Patellofemoral pain syndrome
- This may also be abbreviated to PFPS and is more commonly known as Runner Knee.
- This is a highly common running injury, affecting 1/4 of male runners.[^1]
- It is caused by problems in the alignment of the knee joint, and weakness in the muscles in the hip and thigh.[^2]

### Foot Tendonitis

> Foot tendonitis is inflammation in any of the tendons in your foot. The most common are the Achilles, peroneal, extensor and posterior tibial tendons. An irritated foot tendon is usually the result of overuse and can lead to pain and swelling.[^3]

**The symptoms of foot tendonitis:**
 - [bone spurs](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/10395-bone-spurs-osteophytes).
 - pain that gets worse with exercise.
 - stiffness after inactivity, such as in the morning.
 - thickening of the tendon.
 - swelling, redness or warmth around the tendon.

This is important to pay attention to, because overstretching or a sudden injury *can* lead to a tendon rupture. 

**To prevent this injury:**
 - Maintain a healthy BMI.
 - Don't push through pain. Pay attention to your body.
 - Rest between workouts.
 - Gradually work up to intense physical activity.
 - Stretching before and after physical activity.
 - Use proper running form.
 - Wear supportive, well-fitting shoes.

### Shin Splints

Medial Tibial Stress Syndrome (MTTS) more commonly known as shin splints is an injury that affects the tibia. The literature is divided as to if this is a stress reaction stress reaction in the distal third of the tibia (shin bone) or inflammation at the muscle-bone interface.

![[tibia diagram.png|400]]

**The symptoms[^4] of this largely overlap with foot tendonitis:**
 - Morning stiffness
 - Pain before or during a run
 - Tenderness over the medial inside area of the heel bone

**To prevent this injury:**
- Slowly increase mileage
- Always warm up, ensuring the [tibialis anterior](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK513304/) is also targeted.
- Calf raises. Also add eccentric calf raises.
- Bodyweight squats.
- Cross training
- Wear correct shoes
- Focus on form
- Check bone density
- Run on soft surfaces
