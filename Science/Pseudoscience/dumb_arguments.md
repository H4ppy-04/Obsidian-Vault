Dumb argument 1: You can't say the limits of space because you haven't been there.

Explanation: This is just down right fallacious. We don't need to physically go
somewhere to analyze its properties. We haven't been to other celestial bodies
either, but that doesn't mean we can't extensively observe them, even
determining their composition using spectroscopy.

Dumb (argument) 2: is the universe a ball or is the universe flat?

We're not actually fully sure. If the universe has zero overall curvature, then
space would go on infinitely in all directions, like a flat plane extended into 3D.
Current CMB measurements suggest that the universe is very close to flat, which is the
current leading theory.
