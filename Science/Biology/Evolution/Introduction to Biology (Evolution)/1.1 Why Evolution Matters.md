
- Examining the remains of living things helps us piece together the sequence of events that occurred from the origin of life to what exists today.
	- Fossils fill in gaps of what happened "in between"
- Evolution is occurring today.
	- Living things respond to their environment
	- They adapt to their environments
- Some things are very difficult to treat because they adapt and evolve rapidly such as bacterial infections of viruses. 
	- When we kill some bacteria that is susceptible to antibiotics, we are implicitly encouraging evolution to take place when  some resistant strains bacteria naturally builds up resistance to the antibiotics.
