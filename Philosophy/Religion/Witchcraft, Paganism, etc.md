### Witchcraft Basics

- Hollywood makes witchcraft look evil, but that’s not accurate.
- What counts as witchcraft depends on the book or tradition you follow.
- Always **do your research**.
- Many everyday acts could be seen as witchcraft (like making a wish and blowing out birthday candles).
- Manipulating fire or energy is real, but takes years of training.
- The most beginners might achieve is making a candle flame flicker or relight.

---

### Recommended Books

- **The Green Witch** – Erin Murphy-Hiscock
- **The Encyclopedia of 5000 Spells** – A large collection of spells with history, origins, and explanations.
- Avoid books that include **closed practices** (like Hoodoo or Voodoo).
    - These belong to specific cultural or ethnic groups.
    - You can only practice them if you are part of that group or have been formally initiated.
- Anyone can practice witchcraft, but **respect closed traditions**.
- Don’t buy **white sage** or **palo santo** — both are closed or endangered.
    - White sage is sacred to Indigenous people and is also becoming rare.

---

### Can Witchcraft Backfire?

- **Always do a protection spell first.**
- “Backfiring” isn’t real, but spells can fail if done incorrectly.
- Be in a good mental state before doing any work.
- Witchcraft is a **practice**, not a religion.

---

### Wicca vs. Witchcraft

- Don’t confuse witchcraft with **Wicca**.
- Wicca is a **religion** founded in the 1950s.
- It’s popular, but blends newer pagan ideas.
- Avoid using Wiccan sources if you’re not practicing Wicca.
- Wicca has roots in racism and portrays deities poorly.
- Wiccans avoid hexing or cursing because it’s against their beliefs.

---

### Paganism

- **Hellenic pagans** worship ancient Greek gods.
- There are many forms of paganism.
- Not all pagans practice witchcraft.
- You don’t have to be pagan to practice witchcraft.

---

### Demons

- The word _demon_ comes from the Greek _daimon_, meaning “spirit” or “lesser deity.”
- In ancient Greek belief, demons weren’t necessarily evil.
- Angels were partly inspired by Greek _daimons_.
- Many types of demons exist — most aren’t evil.
- Some are harmful spirits, but most are neutral or helpful.
- In witchcraft, demons are seen more as **lesser deities**, not embodiments of evil.
