Muslims believe generally that the final revelation from God was communicated to the prophet Muhammad in around the 7th century. They believe that they should also follow the teachings of Muhammad in the form of the Hadith's which is a book that contains all of the important things that Muslims believe he said. Furthermore, they believe that the Qur'an is the direct word of God and God literally spoke in Arabic (Qur'anic Arabic) to communicate his teachings into the Qur'an. They also believe that Muhammad flew to heaven on a winged horse, the moon was split into two, the sun rose from the west, and a bunch of other miracles as per the Qur'an as well. 


## Denominatins

Like [[Interpretation of Scripture|Christianity]] there are different denominations of Islam. They are divided into two main branches, Sunni and Shia. 

### Sunni
Sunni is by far the most popular; it represents about 85-90% of all Muslims. They believe in the tradition (*Sunnah*) of the Prophet and accept community consensus for leadership, recognizing the first four caliphs as rightful early leaders. There are four "sub-schools" that follow four major schools of religious law (*Madhhabs*) including Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali.

### Shia

This represents around 10 to 13% of the global Muslim population. They believe that leadership belonged exclusively to Ali ibn Abi Talib (the Prophet's cousin and son-in-law) and his direct descendants through a divinely guided line of Imams. The subsects include Twelver (*Ithna Ashariyya*) which is the largest group; it is following twelve Imams, Ismaili which follows a different line of hereditary Imams, and Zaydi, primarily located in Yemen, which is differing on the sequential line of Imams.
