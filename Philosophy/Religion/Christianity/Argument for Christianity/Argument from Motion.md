P1. In all things there is a ratio of act and potency
P2. In this world we observe things that are in motion
P3. No moved thing can go into motion unless it is moved by something else
P4. Motion exists in causal chains or in causal series.
P5. Such chains cannot regress to infinity; there must be some first mover because motion only occurs when something else moved it. If there is no unmoved mover then the chain would never begin in the first place.
P6. Since this mover is unmoved it has never gone from potency to act and is therefore pure act.
P7. Since there is no potency in this being there is no capacity for it to be any way other than it is; implying there is no imperfection or limit, thus it is full of knowledge, power, and goodness.
C. Therefore this being is identical to the God of classical theism.


Potency = a real, passive capacity for receiving an act or perfection, sitting strictly between pure nothingness and fully actualized being.
Act = The fulfillment of potency (Non being to being)
Motion = Something being reduced from potency to actuality
