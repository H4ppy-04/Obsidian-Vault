The Euthyphro dilemma generally undermines what God commands.

"Does god command something because it is good or because god is good?"

 - If god commands it because it is good then the goodness does not come from god, meaning that goodness is external to his nature.
 - If god commands it because god is good then goodness is arbitrary because there is no reason why god can't command something that would appear to be evil and then it just 'becomes good' because god commanded it.

God commands some very sketchy things in the bible. For instance, In 1 Samuel 15 God commands Samuel to slaughter the Amalekites, including men, women, children, and infant.
