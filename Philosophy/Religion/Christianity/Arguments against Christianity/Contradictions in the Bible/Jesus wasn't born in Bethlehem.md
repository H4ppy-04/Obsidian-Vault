### A blatant Contradiction

![[discord_contradiction.png]]

**Matthew 2:1**

> "Now when Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judaea in the days of Herod the king, behold, there came wise men from the east to Jerusalem."


**Luke 2:2**

> "And this taxing was first made when Cyrenius [Quirinius] was governor of Syria."

So:

- Matthew places Jesus’s birth during **Herod the Great’s reign** (before 4 BCE).
- Luke ties it to **Quirinius’s census** (6 CE).

==This is a *10 year gap.*==
