
### A priori

Literally *"before experience"* 
**Something you can know just by thinking, without needing to look at the world.**
#### Example

_All bachelors are unmarried._ You don’t need to go check bachelors; the definition makes it true.

### A posteriori

Literally *"after experience"* 
**Something you only know by checking or experiencing the world.**

#### Example

_It’s raining outside._ You can’t know this without looking out the window or checking the weather.
