
## Valid
The structure of the syllogism is correct. (The conclusion follows from the premises)

Example:

P1. All unicorns are magical
P2. Applejack is a unicorn
C. Applejack is magical

It's valid in structure, but unicorns don't exist so the premises aren't sound.

## Sound
The premises are true (sound) and the structure is valid. 

P1. All humans are mortal 
P2. Socrates was a human 
C. Socrates was mortal

Note that all of these syllogisms I have provided, have valid [[Inference Rules]] and are therefore valid in their inferences.
