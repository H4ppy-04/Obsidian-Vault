Sometimes just called concept swapping, it's the idea where an arguer presents an argument that is easy to attack (the motte) whilst simultaneously concealing a much more contentious and harder to defend position (the bailey). When the motte is attacked the arguer retreats to the bailey, allowing the arguer to claim that their position has not been refuted because the attacker has critiqued the motte and not the bailey. It's a fundamentally dishonest tactic that's used more commonly that one might think.
