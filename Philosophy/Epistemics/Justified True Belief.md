
This was an idea that was originally considered by Plato but actually formalized by Edmund Gettier in 1961. It is a way of defining what knowledge is.

### Justification
There is evidence to support the belief. 
### True
What is false cannot be known. Eighty-four is not a prime number. Consequently, it could never be the case that anyone knows that eighty-four is a prime number. One can only know things that are true.

For this reason, the traditional theory of knowledge includes, as one of the components for the analysans for _S knows that P_, the condition that it is true that _P_.

### Belief
You can only know what you believe -> Failing to believe something precludes knowing it.
