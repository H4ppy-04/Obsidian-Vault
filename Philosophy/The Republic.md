On justice (Socrates talks with Cephalus, Polemarchus, and Thrasymachus):
 - Is justice telling the truth and paying your debts? (Cephalus)
	 - Probably not, because if you borrow a weapon from a friend who then becomes insane, should you return it? Probably not. So justice is not just this.
 - Is justice helping your friends and harming your enemies? (Polemarchus)
	 - Would a genuinely just person make people worse by harming them?
	 - If justice makes people worse, then justice can't inherently involve harming people
	 - So then a just person shouldn't harm anyone, even an enemy. Which seems odd.
 - Justice is what benefits the stronger (Thrasymachus)
