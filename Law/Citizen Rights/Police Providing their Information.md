
Police must provide **their information** for if they are exercising a power (per section 202 of LEPRA). The information is:
 - Their name and place of duty
 - The reason for the exercise of power. 

### How soon?

They must do it *as soon as is reasonably practical to do so* or in the case of a [[Lawful Order]] or direction, they must do it **before** making the direction, requirement, or request.
