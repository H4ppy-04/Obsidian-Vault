# On Logic
## Why it sucks.

It's like saying:

> You can only drive this car if you acknowledge where it came from, the factory it was made in, who made it, and the serial number of the steering wheel.

It's completely ridiculous. We're perfectly capable of using logic without reference to a God, nor is it necessary that it must be from a God.

### "Logic comes from god"

"Logic is eternal, immutable, and constant, outside of human subjectivity, which are all the things that god possesses."

Ok - Gold is yellow. The sun looks yellow. That doesn’t mean the sun is made of gold.
