### Introduction

- You **will** get hurt in life and experience adversity. Once you acknowledge that you can start preparing for it.
- Resilience doesn't come from theory it comes from **practice** and **repetition**.
	- Repetition callouses the mind.
- Most people die not knowing what they're truly capable of.
- Deliberately avoid a victims mentality. Even if you're dealt a bad hand.
- ==Even the best pep talk or motivational video is a temporary fix. It won't rewire your brain.==

>[!quote] Heraculatus, 5000 BC
> Out of every 100 men on the battlefield 10 shouldn't even be there. 80 are just targets, nine are the real fighters and we are lucky to have them for they make the battle. Ah, but the one. One is a warrior.

- Learn to love what you fear.
- "Motivation is crap". It comes and it goes. When the bills are paid, the sun is out, everything's in it's nice place. Those who find more than motivation, like drive or obsession, will adapt to challenges, not admit defeat, because at that point you don't care what's in front of you.
- Life won't be perfect but that should not derail you from doing what you need to get better.
