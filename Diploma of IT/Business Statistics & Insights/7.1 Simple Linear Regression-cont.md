#### Correlation Coefficient aka $r$ 
In the case that there is a positive correlation then $r\gt0$. If there is a negative correlation then $r\lt0$. But if there is no linear relationship then $r=0$. 


$$r=\sqrt{R^2}$$
and $$(r)^2=R^2$$

$$0\le R^2 \le 1$$

>[!remember]
>$r=\pm 0.9$ so sign shows the direction and numerical value shows the strength.
>- Comment on the strength based on the $\vert r \vert$ and the strength with the sign $\pm$.


When you comment on lowercase $r$ always talk about the:
- direction 
- strength
If the sign is positive its a positive correlation, negative sign is negative correlation.

A strong vs moderate vs weak relation is as follows (copy onto #wsta1250/final  cheatsheet)

$0.7 \le \vert r \vert \le 1$ there is a strong correlation
$0.4 \le \vert r \vert  \lt 0.7$ there is a moderate correlation
$0 \le \vert r \vert \lt 0.4$ there is a weak correlation.
