A sports scientist wants to know if a high-velocity power training program ($G_1$) produces higher peak leg extension force (in Newtons) than a traditional heavy resistance program ($G_2$).

They test two groups of athletes:

- **Group 1 (Power Training):** $n_1 = 16$
- **Group 2 (Traditional):** $n_2 = 16$

After 12 weeks, the peak force results are:

- **Group 1 Mean ($\bar{x}_1$):** $1250\text{ N}$
- **Group 2 Mean ($\bar{x}_2$):** $1210\text{ N}$
- **Standard Error ($SE$):** $28.5\text{ N}$
- **Significance Level ($\alpha$):** Set at $0.05$
