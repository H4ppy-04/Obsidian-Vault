**Problem 1.** Shoppers in a city are surveyed by the chamber of commerce. Some of the questions in the survey are listed below. What type of data will result from each of the following questions?
1. What is your age (in years)? (Quantitative continuous)
2. Which mode of transport did you use to travel to the city today? (Public, private) (Qualitative nominal)
3. How far did you travel to the city today (In kilometers)? (Quantitative continuous)
4. How much did you spend in the city today? (Quantitative continuous)
5. What did you spend most of your money on (choose one)? (Clothes, shoes, food, electronic goods, services, other) (Qualitative nominal)
6. How satisfied are you with your shopping experience in the city? (Circle one) (Very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, unsatisfied, very unsatisfied.) (Qualitative ordinal)
