## Stopping Distance Exercise

## Revision App Test

Research Question: Do students who use a new revision app score higher on the final exam than the usual average score of 72?

$H_0:\mu=72$, $H_a:\mu\gt 72$ 

$$z=\frac{\bar{y}-\mu_0}{\sigma/\sqrt{n}}$$
**Sample data**: 74, 69, 81, 77, 73, 75, 70, 78, 76, 72, 80, 71, 79, 74, 77, 73

$\bar{y}:$ 74.9375
$\alpha:$ 0.05
$n$: 16
$\sigma$: 8 (marks)
$SE$: 2
$z=74.9375-72/2=2.9375/2=1.46875$
$p=0.07095$

Conclusion: The P value value is less than the significance level of 0.05 and so there is insufficient evidence to reject the null hypothesis.

## Battery Question

A battery manufacturer claims that its rechargeable batteries have an average lifespan of **1,000 charge cycles**.

An independent laboratory wants to determine whether the true average lifespan is **different** from the manufacturer's claim.

Previous testing has established that the **population standard deviation is 40 charge cycles**.

A random sample of **36 batteries** is tested.

### Given

- $\mu_0​=1000$ charge cycles
- $\sigma$=40
- $n=36$
- α=0.05

We can also say:
- $H_0:\mu=1,000$
- $H_a:\mu\neq 1,000$

$\bar{y}:995.5556$
$SE:-4.4444$
$z:991.1112$
$p:0.544235$

Since $p=0.544235<0.05$ we fail to reject $H_0$. There is insufficient evidence to conclude that the true mean charge cycles is 1000 charges.
