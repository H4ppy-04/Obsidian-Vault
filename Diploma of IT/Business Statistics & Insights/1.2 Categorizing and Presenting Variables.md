### Types of Variables

| Categorical                                                                                   | Numerical                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ordinal**. These are values that have some order to them. For example income or age groups. | **Discrete**. These are variables that are countable and so necessarily integers.                                 |
| **Nominal**. These are values that do not have any order to them, such as race or gender.     | **Continuous**. These are variables that are not strictly integers as they may be represented in some other form. |

**Summarize:** Re-organize the data into a table.
**Presenting:** Usually a form of graphical display.

How to summarize categorical variable:
1. Count: the number of observation that falls into each category.
2. Proportions and percentages (optional?)

Types of graphical displays:
1. ==Bar chart==
2. Pie chart

>[!tip]
>In this unit, **bar charts** are preferred over pie charts.


Remember: ==total of a table is always the sample size==
