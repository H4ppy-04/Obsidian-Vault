A statistical test, or **hypothesis test**, is a process involving a series of formal steps.

A hypothesis test should follow these steps:

**Hypotheses**: State $H_0$ and $H_a$ in terms of the *parameter* of interest.
**Assumptions:** Check the underlying assumptions of the test.
**Test statistic:** Calculate the test statistic.
**P-value**: Obtain the P-value from the test distribution of the test statistic.
**Decision:** If the P-value is less than $\alpha$, reject $H_0$. If the P-value is not less than $\alpha$, do not reject $H_0$. 
**Conclusion**: Write a conclusion to the original research question in terms of the target population.
