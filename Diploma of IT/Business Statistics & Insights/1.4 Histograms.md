### Histograms
 - On the horizontal axis is always the numerical variable. You have the range of the numerical variable.
 - On the vertical axis is always the frequency
	 - frequency means the *count*
 - Bar with the greatest height is where the *mode* is. (most frequent observation)


#### Skewed histograms
 - Don't look at the direction the histogram is facing
 - If the LHS 'tail' extending all the way to the left then it's a left-skewed histogram.
 - Same for right-skewed histogram.
 - Look at the side of the tail that is being extended.
 - Need to know for class test.

Both the X and Y are numerical variables.
Lines that slope up is a positive linear relationship (as x goes up, y goes up)
Lines that slope down is a negative linear relation (as x goes up, y goes down)
A straight line would be just **no linear relation**

Any curve or parabola is **non-linear**
