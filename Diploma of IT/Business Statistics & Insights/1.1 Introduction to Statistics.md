#### Criterion of a *Sample*

1. Must share *similar characteristics* with the *target population*, and is not *biased* towards a specific characteristic.
2. Sufficiently large to give accurate information about the *target population*. Typically a value of $n \ge 25$ and where respondents are $\ge 75\%$. 
3. To select a sample that is reflective of the *target population*, the sample should be a *random sample*.
4. When describing a *sample* ensure to always include the *sample size*. The *sample size* is denoted by the character $n$.

#### Observational vs Experimental Studies

1. An <u>observational study</u> has *no intervention* by the investigator, nor is there any *treatment*.
2. An <u>experimental study</u> permits the investigator to have *some control* of the *determinant*.
