# Formulas

$$
\bar{y} = \frac{y_1 + y_2+\ldots\ldots\ldots+y_n}{n}
$$
$$
\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}x_i}{n}
$$
$$
s = \sqrt{\frac{(y_1-\bar{y})^2+(y_2-\bar{y})^2+\ldots\ldots+(y_n-\bar{y})^2}{n-1}}
$$
$$IQR=UQ-LQ$$
$$LF=LQ-1.5\times IQR$$
$$UF=UQ+1.5\times IQR$$
$$Range=max-min$$
$$
SE=\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}
$$
$$t=\frac{\bar{x}-\mu}{\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}}$$

$$
\sum(x-\mu) = 0
$$

$$
\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum(x-\mu)^2}{N}
$$
