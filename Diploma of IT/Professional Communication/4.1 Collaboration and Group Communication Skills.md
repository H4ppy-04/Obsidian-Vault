Overall lesson purpose: Provide guidelines and tips on how to effectively collaborate and present as a group. Covering submission requirements, presentation techniques and group dynamics. 

1. Assessment due date & time 
    
2. How to submit and present your assessments 
    
3. Elements of effective group presentations 
    
4. Group work and portfolio check in. 
    

Preparing group presentation 

- Plan and preparation 
    
- Cohesion and collaboration 
    
- Practicing and presenting 
    

Sections 

- Introduction 
    
    - Greet the audience 
        
    - Introduce the speakers 
        
    - Tell the audience when they can ask questions 
        
    - Start with a speech hook 
        
        - Ask a thought provoking or rhetorical question connected to your topic 
            
        - Use a powerful quote or headline 
            
        - Telling a captivating story (don't do this; consider time left) 
            
        - Presenting an interesting fact or statistic 
            
        - Showing an interesting prop, visual, or short video 
            
        - Using an interactive task (not suitable) 
            
            - Ask the audience to do something. 
                
        - Creating suspense.  
            
        - Activating the audiences imagination. 
            
- Main body 
    
- Conclusion 
    

For team cohesion: 

- Ensure that we all contribute equally 
    
- We divide speaking content equally 
    
- Are aware of each-others content 
    
- Have practiced delivering the presentation together 
    
- Use 'we' rather than 'I' when introducing the topic and purpose of the presentation 
- Use signpost language when leaving and accepting the floor for a smooth transition.
