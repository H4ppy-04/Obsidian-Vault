Why am I being asked to write an essay? 

- Professional writing is a form of writing used for a specialized topic. 
    
    - Specialized fields include IT, medicine, law, etc. It can be on any field. 
        
- IT: 
    
    - Instruction manuals 
        
    - Install guides 
        
    - Etc. 
        
- Reasons to write with cohesion and precision 
    
    - To persuade (professional, academic, etc.) 
        
- After choosing essay: 
    
    - Contextualize your topic. 
        
- You would use convincing and precise language to present evidence that shows that soft skills have a positive effect on professional outcomes. 
    
- Taking a position 
    
    - Form an opinion 
        
    - Begin to see similar evidence supported in several sources 
        
        - The more you read the better. 
            
    - Begin to see contrasting evidence 
        
        - Which leads you to think... what do I want to say? 
            
        - Minimize bias 
            
    
    1. Choose a side. This is your thesis statement. 
        
    2. Support your position, use facts examples, statistics, etc. 
        
    3. Address counter-arguments 
        
        1. Your position "acknowledges" and "refutes" counter evidence. 
            
    4. Maintain clarity and consistency 
        
        1. Avoid contradicting yourself 
            
        2. Use clear and logical communication, helps your readers follow your argument. 
            

1. How to recognize bias 
    
    1. Gender bias. "Women can't handle stress as well as men" (unfair stereotype) 
        
    2. Occupational bias (He so good at math's for an artist) 
        
    3. Confirmation bias (I only read from sources that align with my political views) 
        
    4. Implicit bias (They should stick to their kind) 
        
    5. Anchoring bias (The first price I saw for this car was 30k so 20k seems like a great deal) 
        
2. Ordering your research notes 
    
    1. Look at your sources (books, journals, government org., etc.) 
        
    2. Make sure that the referencing corresponds to the media type 
        
    3. Discern which evidence is most relevant (see research grid in resources) 
        
3. Arrange evidence in order of weight 
    
    1. First idea or argument 
        
        1. Evidence 1 – statistic, quote definition 
            
        2. Evidence 2 – statistic, quote definition 
            
        
    2. Second idea or argument 
        
        1. Evidence 1 – statistic, quote definition 
            
        2. Evidence 2 – statistic, quote definition 
            
    
    3. Third idea or argument 
        
        1. Evidence 1 – statistic, quote definition 
            
        2. Evidence 2 – statistic, quote definition
