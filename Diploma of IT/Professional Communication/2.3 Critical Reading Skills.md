This lesson emphasizes the importance of critical reading, which involves evaluating the writer's position, evidence, and methods. It encourages readers to ask questions such as: 

- What position is the writer presenting? 
    
- What evidence supports this position? 
    
- Is the evidence accurate and collected appropriately? 
    
- Are the writer's claims reasonable? 
    
- How does the writer's position compare with others in the field? 
    

Case Study: Is Tuvalu Sinking?
