Definition of AI: It is a simulation of human intelligence. Programmed to think like humans. 

They can do all sorts of things like visual analysis, problem solving, etc. 

There are 2 different categories: 

1. Narrow AI (weak AI) - Designed to do a limited number of tasks. 
    
2. General AI (Strong AI) - Broader; it can do a wide range of tasks (LLM's, etc.) 
    

AI And Professional Communication 

- Enhanced personalization 
    
- Improved efficiency 
    
- Better collaboration 
    
- Data driven insights 
    

Ethical Considerations of AI 

- Bias – Preference towards various outcomes 
    
- Privacy – Ranging on a wide range of data. Personal information should be kept private and secure. 
    
- Accountability – Who is accountable for decisions made by AI? 
    
- Transparency – Ensuring that information is open to the public 
    

AI has revolutionized education. 

The ways that AI is improving education is: 

- Synthesizing information quickly. 
    
- Personalized learning – Analyze learning patterns and adapt education content to meet their needs. 
    
- Intelligent tutoring systems – Provides student with support outside the classroom. 
    
- Enhance accessibility – Making education more accessible with students with disabilities. 
    

Disadvantages: 

- Bias and fairness – Perpetuate bias and training data (race, gender, etc.) 
    
- Privacy concerns – Students may share sensitive/personal information 
    
- Lack of transparency – The decision making process is a black box. 
    
- Dependent on technology – Affects critical thinking skills (decision making, etc.) 
    
- Digital divide – not all students have equal access to technology 
    
- Ethical concerns – The use of AI in education raises ethical concerns (potential of AI to replace human teachers, implications it may have on students futures) 
    

Practical problems in education: 

-  Hallucinations – it gives information and sources that don't exist. 
    
- Biased – it learns from vast amounts of data. 
    
- Security and misuse – it can be misused for unethical usage. 
    

Don't use AI to cheat. 

How can AI be used in education?     - Summarizing content 

-  Language translator 
    
- TTS / STT 
    
- Feedback 
    
- Ask it to explain different things 
    
- Quiz generation – Personalize based on your needs and provides instant feedback. 
    

Summarizing content:  

-  - it can summarize complex issues quickly 
    
-  It can make education resource in their own language, however there is a drawback of oversimplification.  
    

 Drawbacks: 

-  Oversimplification (loses details or nuance, leading to shallow understanding of material) 
    
- AI may mis-interpret information. 
    

Speech to text:  

-  AI can convert speech to text or vice versa 
    
- Benefit is that it makes different sources accessible 
    
- Easier transcription of videos / lectures 
    
- Also helps international students improve language skills 
    

Drawbacks: 

1. Accuracy – Speech to text might be inaccurate depending on background noise, accent, technical jargon, etc. 
    
2. Text to speech may sound un-natural which can affect engagement. 
    

AI copilot exercise  

-  The AI largely summarized the information effectively, however I noticed that it made several inferences and based off of that, gave certain information I didn't ask. For example, I told it that I was a student at Macquarie university and it gave me practical takeaways that were specific to students and educators at Macquarie university. This was likely done in an offer to be helpful, however it means that the AI was taking initiative where I had not necessarily asked it to. 
    
- There were no "impossible questions" that couldn't be answered. 
    
- When contrasting between quizzes that had 5 vs 20 questions, quizzes that contained 20 questions contained some questions that were less important or applicable that quizzes that contained 5 questions. This may be because quizzes that contain 5 questions have the opportunity to be more content-specific and focused on the key information in the study.
