Different Types of Reflection 

Reflective thinking 

- Thinking about ones beliefs, assumptions, etc. 
    
- Replaying events, conversations, feelings, etc. 
    

Reflective Practice 

- John Dewey defines this as "active persistent and careful consideration of any belief or supposed form of knowledge, in the light of the grounds that support it." 
    
- Questioning why things are the way they are and how they could be different. 
    
- It helps us to be conscious of our biases 
    
- It encourages to look at things from different perspectives 
    
- It helps us avoid past mistakes and challenges. 
    

Reflective Writing Components 

1. Describe the task and what your feelings were. 
    
2. Analysis. Critically analyze the factors undertaking the experience. 
    
3. Future actions. Aim to identify and explain strategies for future practice.

Time I didn't do well on an assignment: 

Description: There was an assignment that was due for a given course in 7th grade. It was about analyzing rock formations. We were instructed to identify different rock types and explain how they had formed. 

Analysis: I felt the task didn't have any applicability and was thus demotivated to complete the task. I put minimal effort into the task and then received a failing grade. 

 Future strategies: In future I should find external motivation to complete tasks regardless of applicability. This should result me in receiving better grades.
