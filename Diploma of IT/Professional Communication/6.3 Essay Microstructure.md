Topic sentence: Must be in the author's voice. 

Supporting sentences: come after topic sentence. Functions include: 

 - Provide more detail 

 - Breaks down essential elements 

 - Introduces evidence in the form of sources and citations (more likely) 

 - provide explanation, reasoning, or justification 

-  Not all supporting sentences need citations 
    

Transitional sentence 

-  - Transition to an adjacent aspect of the main topic sentence 
    
-  Shows a transition to the reader: "I'm going to focus on another aspect" 
    
- Usually one sentence. 
    
- Connects ideas between supporting sentences. 
    
- Not all paragraphs require transitional sentences.(Eg if you only have to talk about one aspect of the topic sentence) 
    
- All in the writer own voice 
    

Concluding sentence: 

- Link the main ideas in the body paragraph to the thesis statement. Explain how it's related. 
    
- May establish a link to the next paragraph. 
    
- Ensure that this is in the writers voice (no citations; they should only come in supporting sentences) Should only have 1 concluding sentence. 
    
- Concluding is just one function. 
    
- Needs to restate the topic sentence.
