
UNSCOP was[ created May 15 1947](https://unispal.un.org/UNISPAL.NSF/0/F5A49E57095C35B685256BCF0075D9C2) in response to a UK request that the General Assembly "make recommendations under article 10 of the Charter, concerning the future government of Palestine"
The purpose was to recommend how the British Mandate territory should be governed in future.

- [UNSCOP visited Palestine](https://web.archive.org/web/20150910184406/http://unispal.un.org/UNISPAL.NSF/0/F8467BB39088EAD085256E9900513CC2) and gathered testimony from Zionist organizations in Palestine and in the US.
- The Arab Higher Committee boycotted the commission, saying that Palestinian Arabs' natural rights were self evident and as such could not continue to be subject to investigation.
- The [report](https://web.archive.org/web/20120603150222/http://domino.un.org/unispal.nsf/9a798adbf322aff38525617b006d88d7/07175de9fa2de563852568d3006e10f3?OpenDocument) on 3 September 1947 supported the **termination of the British Mandate**, containing a majority proposal for a two-state partition plan with:
	- An economic union
	- Minority proposal for a plan for a federal union with Jerusalem as the capital.
