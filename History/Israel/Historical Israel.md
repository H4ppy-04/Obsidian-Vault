Prior to 1948, there was a community of Jews - the Yishuv (ישוב which means "settlement") - residing in Palestine prior to the establishment of the State of Israel. This community may also be referred to as the Palestinian-Jewish community (Divine & D. R., 2004). The term came into use in the 1880's where there were about about 25,000 Jews living in that region, and then by 1948 there were around 630,000 Jews living in the region (Ben Rafael et al., 1991)

Israel has continually needed to fight for its survival among hostile surrounding Arab neighbors. Here's a brief timeline of the opposition to the State of Israel:

**1919**
1. Emir Faisal bin Al-Hussein, leader of the Great Arab Revolt during WWI, signed an agreement with Chaim Weizmann, President of the Zionist Organization, calling for cooperation between "the Arab State and Palestine." Interestingly, Emir considered Palestinians to mean Jews then. (source required  )
2. Furthermore, To Felix Frankfurter of the American Zionist delegation Faisal wrote: _"We Arabs, especially the educated among us, look with the deepest sympathy on the Zionist movement" and "We wish the Jews a most hearty welcome home."_ (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Letter_to_Felix_Frankfurter_written_by_T._E._Lawrence_in_the_name_of_Prince_Faisal,_March_1919.jpg) (https://blogs.timesofisrael.com/in-search-of-an-arab-israeli-modus-vivendi/) (https://jewishvirtuallibrary.org/feisal-frankfurter-correspondence-march-1919)
##### 1920's:
1. Beginning with the [[1917 Balfour Declaration]], this was opposed almost immediately, with Arab inhabitants of Mandatory Palestine establishing Muslim-Christian Associations in all of the major towns. Ira M. Lapidus, _A History of Islamic Societies_, 2002: "The first were the nationalists, who in 1918 formed the first Muslim-Christian associations to protest against the Jewish national home" p.558
2. At the same time as all of this, the Zionist commission formed in March 1918 and actively promoted Zionist objectives in Palestine. Following this in April of 1920, elections took place for the Assembly of Representatives of the Palestinian Jewish community.
3. In the very same month, the beginning of the Nebi-Musa riots in Jerusalem took place and in the month of April there were the deaths of five Jews and four Arabs.
4. In May 1921, there were then riots with almost 100 dying in the violent Jaffa Riots which began as a confrontation between two Jewish groups but developed into an attack by Arabs con Jews and then reprisal attacks by Jews on Arabs.
