 - There have been heavy restrictions on the movement of people and other resources in and out of Gaza (OCHA, 2026). However, this does not justify terrorist action by Hamas. According to Pamment et al. (2019), Hamas is a terrorist organization that is "the largest, most prominent, and most military developed organization in the Gaza Strip" (p. 152). Pamment et al. (2019) further argue that Hamas has been using Palestinian civilians as human shields. The report states that, in response, Israel has limited its use of force, usually only firing near civilians if there is an immediate threat, as well as using various warning methods and dropping non-lethal explosives (Pamment et al., 2019).
