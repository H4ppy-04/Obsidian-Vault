For Israeli People this is the **War of Independence**.
For Palestinians they call it the Nakba (The Great Displacement)

This was a war that occurred between Palestinian-Arab and Jewish forces that happened whilst under the British Mandate. 

The rejection and attempted implementation of the [[1947 Partition Plan]] triggered a civil war between Palestinian-Arab and Jewish forces in late 1947. After Israel declared independence in May 1948, neighboring Arab armies intervened, beginning the interstate phase.

For context, surrounding Arab states recruited Palestinians and other Syrian fighters and volunteers and a liberation movement was created shortly after, with Arab decenters of the United Nations vote about the Partition plan saying "This will lead to war" - which was not an idle threat.
