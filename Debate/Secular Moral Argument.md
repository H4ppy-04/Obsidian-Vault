
P1. We all have the right to freedom and wellbeing
P2. Any action that violates ones freedom and wellbeing violates those rights
P3. If an action violates ones rights it is immoral
C. Therefore, taking actions that violates others rights is immoral


Argument for P1:
P1. I am an agent (I take actions to achieve goals)
P2. In order to achieve any of my goals I need my freedom and wellbeing which means I need for you to not intefere with that
P3. Which means that I have a right to it. 
P4. If I have a right to my freedom and wellbeing on the basis that I am an agent then I must be logically consistent and say that all agents have  a similar right
C. All agents have a right to freedom and wellbeing


There are notable exceptions under the kantian view for instance the forfeiting of ones rights should they intefere with others rights.
