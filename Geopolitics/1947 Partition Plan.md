Also known as UN Resolution 181 follows the partition plan after the termination of the British Mandate for Palestine to establish the division of Arab and Jewish states.

>[!note]
> 
*Apparently* (and this is largely unverified) - there were internal documents (letters, cabinet meeting transcripts, Ben-Gurion's journal, etc.), that showed that the Yeshuv were going to accept the partition plan publicly but lied. 
Of note, there is some evidence to this in some journal entries, however it is often used to suggest that there was some "sinister" motive, but this is not true.


Link to the actual partition plan UN Resolution 181: https://www.inss.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/All-UN-Resolutions.pdf
Link to original map of partition plan: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/3792734?ln=en
