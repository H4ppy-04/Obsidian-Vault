A Palestinian child, once youthful spirit now crushed,
screams for mercy without rest.
He watches, confused, in agony—
a tear slide down the broken face of his mother,
the shattered innocence in his bloodied sister’s eyes.
Both bleed on the broken road
of a city that hasn’t slept,
a people who haven’t stopped.

And children watch,
in utter helplessness,
as death walks by.
