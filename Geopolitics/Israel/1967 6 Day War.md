The 6 day war was a conflict in 1967 between Israel and a coalition of Arab nations (primarily Egypt, Syria and Jordan) in the broader Arab-Israeli conflict.

### Why it started

 - There is not really "one main reason" as to why it started but rather being a mix of factors.
 - Israel and it's neighbors post-1948 were still in a state of conflict
	 - Recurring border clashes
	 - Palestinian guerilla/fedayeen attacks with then retaliatory action by Israel
	 - Arms race
	 - Arab governments stating they did not accept Israel's legitimacy
 - Prior to this in 1966-1967 the tensions between **Syria** and Israel were high
	 - Syria supported Palestinian attacks
	 - Israel responded defensively
	 - **In April 1967 a major Israeli-Syrian clash involved an air battle where Israel shot down six Syrian MiGs** (https://history.state.gov/milestones/1961-1968/arab-israeli-war-1967)
 - **The immediate reason was the soviet intelligence claim**
	 - The story goes that on the 13th of May 1967 the Soviet Union told Syria and Egypt that Israel had massed forces on the Syrian border and was preparing an invasion
	 - This was incorrect; U.S intelligence concluded that the alleged concentration did not exist. However, the Soviets believe (or at least propagated) that there was an imminent attack.
	 - Egyptian President **Gamal Abdel Nasser** felt pressure to demonstrate the Egypt would defend Syria and maintain its position as leader of Arab nationalism
