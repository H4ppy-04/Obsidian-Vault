## Legality 

Article III of The Fourth Geneva Convention (1949) prohibits ==violence to life and person== against civilians, including murder.
Article 27 protects civilians in an armed conflict, without discrimination, including "threats to their lives".
International Humanitarian Law (Rule 34) declares journalists as civilians unless they take a direct part in hostilities.
Article 35(3)(a) of the Additional Protocol I (1977) for International Armed Conflicts states the "making of civilians the object of attack" a *grave breach* and a war crime.
The United Nations Security Council Resolution 1738 (2006) specifically *condemns* attacks against journalists in armed conflict.
