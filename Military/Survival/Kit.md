
## [[Water]]

- Water purification tablets. povidone-iodine is especially useful. You can use it to get rid of disease and germ ridden water. 1 drop for 1 liter of water.
- Life straws can be useful. There are some that have a filter that is a microscopic metric  which filters out diseases like E. Coli. 

## Fire

- metal match, waterproof matches, magnesium bar, candle, magnifying lens are all things that help.
- Carrying cotton balls are good because they can compact and are very effective.
