
- A fully grown adult can only last a few days without water and therefore it is essential to survival.
 - A loss of water is normal through bodily processes (sweating, urinating, defecating). On a regular day (20 degrees Celsius) the average adult uses and therefore needs to replace 2-3 Liters of water daily. ==Thirst is not a sign of how much water you need.== It's important to replace and ration water regularly.
- In any situation where food intake is low, drink 6 to 8 liters of water per day.


## Measuring Water Loss

- With a 0.75-liter loss the wrist pulse rate will be under 100 beats per minute and the breathing rate 12 to 20 breaths per minute.
- With a 0.75- to 1.5-liter loss the pulse rate will be 100 to 120 beats per minute and 20 to 30 breaths per minute.
- With a 1.5- to 2-liter loss the pulse rate will be 120 to 140 beats per minute and 30 to 40 breaths per minute. Vital signs above these rates require more advanced care.
