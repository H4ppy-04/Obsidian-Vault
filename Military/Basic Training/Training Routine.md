## **PHYSICAL FITNESS COMPONENTS**

### **1. Push-Ups (Upper Body)**

- Week 1–2: 3–5 sets of 5–10 push-ups (focus on **full range, nose/chest near ground**).
- Week 3–4: 3–5 sets of 12–15 reps.
- Week 5–6: Timed 2-min sets → aim for 20–25 full push-ups.
- Week 7–8: Increase pace, maintain full form, target 25–30+ comfortably.
    

### **2. Sit-Ups (Core)**

- Week 1–2: 3–4 sets of 10–15 reps.
- Week 3–4: 3–5 sets of 20–25 reps.
- Week 5–6: Timed 2-min sets → aim for 40–50 reps.
- Week 7–8: Practice maximum reps in 2 minutes to exceed minimum standard (45).

### **3. Running / Cardio**

- Week 1–2: 1–2 km jog at comfortable pace, 2–3 times/week.
- Week 3–4: 2–3 km, include 1–2 interval sessions (sprints + jog).
- Week 5–6: 3–5 km, add hill/stair runs to simulate field conditions.
- Week 7–8: 2–4 km tempo runs + beep test practice.
- Goal: Be comfortable with **continuous running and passing level 6.1 beep test**.
    

### **4. Load-Bearing March / Hike**

- Start with 5–8 km with backpack (5–10 kg).
- Gradually increase distance to 15–20 km with 10–15 kg.
- Practice proper pack carrying, posture, and pace control.
    

### **5. Core & Full-Body Strength**

- Planks: start 20–30 sec, work up to 1–2 min.
- Squats & lunges: 3–4 sets of 10–20 reps.
- Dips or bench exercises if available.

## **MENTAL & EMOTIONAL PREPARATION**

1. **Early Wake-Up Practice**
    - Wake at 5–6 a.m., even on weekends, to get used to BT schedule.
2. **Simulate Stress**
    - Try push-ups or running after a long day or when slightly sleep-deprived.
3. **Focus & Discipline**
    - Practice following written instructions exactly.
    - Set small daily goals and stick to them.
4. **Stress Coping Techniques**
    - Deep breathing, mindfulness, or short meditation sessions.
    - Helps during sleep deprivation and high-pressure BT drills.

---

## **PRACTICAL PREP & KNOWLEDGE**

- Learn **how to pack a backpack efficiently**.
- Familiarize yourself with **basic first aid and navigation**.
- Research BT routines, uniform care, and military expectations.
- Maintain **nutrition and hydration**, aim for lean protein, complex carbs, and regular water intake.

---
### **Consistency Tips**

- Track reps, runs, and marches in a notebook or app.
- Gradually increase intensity; don’t overdo it early.
- Focus on **form first, speed second**.
- Rest is part of training—don’t skip recovery.
