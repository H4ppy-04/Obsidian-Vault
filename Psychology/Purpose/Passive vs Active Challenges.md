
## Passive Challenges

Passive challenges are things that life throws at you that are unavoidable.

- Paying rent or paying off a mortgage
- Working a job
- Making sure there's food on the table

## Active Challenges

These are challenges that you impose on yourself.

 - I want to learn how to speak another language
 - Learning a new skill like philosophy or a science
 - Increasing physical fitness

When you are overwhelmed by passive challenges the solution is to find an active challenge and pursue that.
